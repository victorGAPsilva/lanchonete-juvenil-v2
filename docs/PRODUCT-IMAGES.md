# Imagens ilustrativas dos produtos

Geradas pelo recurso ImageGen integrado (modo built-in), para produtos fictícios de demonstração. Não são fotografias dos produtos reais da Lanchonete Juvenil. As imagens originais foram inspecionadas, reduzidas para 900 px de largura e codificadas em WebP, qualidade 82, para o cardápio e o carrossel.

Arquivos finais no projeto:

- `public/assets/products/croissant.webp`
- `public/assets/products/hamburguer.webp`
- `public/assets/products/suco.webp`

Prompt comum utilizado, substituindo `{subject}` pelo respectivo assunto abaixo:

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: illustrative demonstration food image for a snack bar digital menu and image carousel. Subject: {subject} Style: appetizing clean studio food photography, soft daylight, realistic natural textures, warm welcoming mood. Composition: landscape 3:2, full product centered with generous empty margins, camera at 35 degree angle, uncluttered solid cream background. Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no hands, no extra food. This is a fictional demonstration product, not a photograph of the actual restaurant's food.
```

Assuntos exatos:

```text
croissant: A golden baked croissant sliced and filled with ham and melted cheese, on a small off-white ceramic plate, warm pale cream tabletop.

hamburguer: A classic Brazilian snack-bar cheeseburger with one grilled beef patty, melted cheese, lettuce, tomato and sesame bun, on a small off-white ceramic plate, warm pale cream tabletop.

suco: A clear glass of orange juice with ice and a fresh orange half beside it, warm pale cream tabletop.
```
